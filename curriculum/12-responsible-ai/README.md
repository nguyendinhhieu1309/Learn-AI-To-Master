# Responsible AI

Responsible AI is a cross-cutting practice for designing, building, and operating systems that affect people. It applies to data collection, evaluation, interfaces, deployment, and maintenance—not only to a final model.

## Outcomes

- Identify affected people, intended use, and plausible harms before modeling.
- Evaluate performance across groups and deployment conditions.
- Reduce privacy and security risks and explain limitations clearly.
- Provide appropriate human review, recourse, and monitoring.
